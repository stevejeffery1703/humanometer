Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

This is the plain-English version. Nothing hidden.

What we do (and don't) collect

If you just take the assessment: nothing leaves your device that identifies you. Your scores live in your browser's session storage and in any permalink you choose to share. We don't track you, set advertising cookies, or pass anything to third parties.

If you tick the opt-in box and submit your email: we store your email address, an optional name, the date you opted in, your IP address, and the country your IP is in. These are kept alongside your opt-in record until you unsubscribe — the IP address and country for spam protection and to understand roughly where our users are. That's it.

If you buy a paid pack: Stripe handles your payment — we never see your card details. Your email and name are passed to Stripe so they can issue a receipt.

Visitor counter: we keep an anonymous count of how many people have taken the assessment. No identifying information is recorded.

Why we collect what we collect

  • Email + name (opt-in): so we can email you when we launch new features. Nothing else. We do not sell, share, or rent.
  • IP / country: to detect spam signups and understand roughly where users are.
  • Payment data via Stripe: to process the transaction and send a receipt.

Where it lives

Email opt-ins are stored in Cloudflare KV, a key-value store on Cloudflare's infrastructure. We are the only party with access. We don't use Mailchimp, Constant Contact, or any other third-party email marketing service.

The site itself is hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which means Cloudflare sees the same web requests any host would (IP, browser, URL). They process this on our behalf as a data processor under their DPA.

How long we keep it

  • Email opt-in: until you unsubscribe.
  • Payment records (via Stripe): as required by tax law (typically 6+ years).
  • Visitor counter: indefinitely — it's a number, not personal data.

Your rights under GDPR

If you're in the EU/UK (or even if you're not), you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Delete your data (the "right to be forgotten")
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
Unsubscribe / delete right now: visit /unsubscribe.html, enter your email, and we'll delete your record immediately. No login, no friction. Or email hello@humanometer.com with any of the above requests.

Cookies and tracking

We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising. We don't load Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Hotjar, or anything similar. Your browser uses session storage to remember your reading between page reloads — that's local to your device and never sent to us.

Children

The Humanometer is intended for working professionals. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the date at the top will change. Material changes will be announced on the homepage.

Contact

Questions, requests, or anything else: hello@humanometer.com
