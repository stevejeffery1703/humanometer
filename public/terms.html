What Humanometer is

Humanometer is a self-assessment tool. You answer 15 timed scenario questions and receive a scored reading across five dimensions, plus optional paid packs that use AI to generate personalised career coaching from your scores — an "edge" reading of how your dimensions combine, your interview blind spots, memory-joggers for your own interview stories, an interview prep guide, a fillable cheat sheet, a LinkedIn "About" draft, a certificate, and a full-results file. The top tier can also tailor a brief to a specific role from a job description you choose to paste in. The coaching is built from your scores (and, where you provide it, that job description) plus general career knowledge; it does not invent facts about you or the employer.
