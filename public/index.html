Frequently asked questions

What is the Humanometer?

A free 5-minute online assessment measuring five professional capabilities AI cannot replicate: Adaptive Thinking, Ethical Judgement, Creative Synthesis, Empathic Accuracy, and Critical Scepticism. You get a scored reading across each dimension — not a single personality label.

Is it free?

Yes. The full assessment, all five dimension scores, development insights, and a shareable results card are completely free. The optional Results Pack (£9.99) generates a LinkedIn-ready summary, personalised interview answers, and a career synthesis from your exact scores.

How is this different from a personality test?

Unlike Myers-Briggs or 16Personalities, the Humanometer is grounded in current labour-market research on which human skills are growing in demand because of AI. It produces five independently scored dimensions rather than a fixed personality type.

How long does it take?

About 5 minutes — 15 questions, each timed at 25–30 seconds to capture instinctive rather than deliberated answers, which research shows are more predictive of genuine capability.
