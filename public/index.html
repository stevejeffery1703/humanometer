Frequently asked questions

What is the Humanometer?

A free 5-minute online assessment measuring five professional capabilities AI cannot replicate: Adaptive Thinking, Ethical Judgment, Creative Synthesis, Empathic Accuracy, and Critical Skepticism. You get a scored reading across each dimension.

Is it free?

Yes. The full assessment, all five dimension scores, development insights, and a shareable results card are completely free. Optional one-time paid packs (from $6.99) turn your scores into real interview coaching — how your strengths combine, the traps to avoid, the stories to prepare and the questions you'll face and how to answer them — plus LinkedIn 'About' themes and a certificate.

How is this different from a personality test?

Unlike Myers-Briggs or 16Personalities, the Humanometer is grounded in current labor-market research on which human skills are growing in demand because of AI. It produces five independently scored dimensions rather than a fixed personality type.

How long does it take?

About 5 minutes — 15 timed scenario questions designed to capture instinctive rather than deliberated answers, which research shows are more predictive of genuine capability.
